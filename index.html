01 / THE PROBLEM

Waste doesn't disappear.
It moves.

When waste is poorly tracked, it can move from streets to drains, waterways and communities — becoming harder to collect, understand and recover.

Too often, decisions happen after the problem is visible. Terranova is built around a different idea: make the invisible visible early enough to act.

01

Fragmented visibility

Waste events happen everywhere, but useful information is rarely connected into one picture.

02

Reactive operations

Collection and response can become a chase instead of a coordinated, data-informed process.

03

Lost value

Materials that could be recovered or redirected are often treated simply as waste.

03 / THE VISION

OUR VISION

A world where waste intelligence becomes environmental action.

Terranova imagines communities where waste is not just collected — it is understood. Where technology helps people see patterns, anticipate pressure points and make better decisions.

We are building toward a future in which the value inside waste is recognised, recovery becomes part of the system, and cleaner environments are supported by better information.
