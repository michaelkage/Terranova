01 / THE CHALLENGE

Plastic doesn't
just disappear.

Plastic waste accumulates in streets, drainage systems, waterways and public spaces. When it enters drains, it can contribute to blocked drainage, flooding, pollution and lost recyclable material.

Terranova focuses on the moment before that waste travels farther: capture it early, understand it, then give the material somewhere better to go.

04 / FROM WASTE TO PRODUCT

Collected plastic
can become value.

The project process identifies several potential recovery pathways depending on material type.

PET

Bottles →

New bottles, polyester fabric/thread, paving tiles and roofing sheets.

HDPE

Containers →

School desks and chairs, bins, crates, pallets, pipes and fittings.

PP

Caps & cups →

Buckets, basins, bowls, battery cases and plastic furniture.

LDPE

Sachets & nylon →

Trash bags, plastic lumber and potential road-material applications.

MIXED

Dirty plastics →

Construction bricks and tiles, roofing sheets and other processing pathways.

05 / THE VISION

WHY TERRANOVA EXISTS

Stop plastic before it becomes a bigger problem.

Terranova exists to prevent plastic waste from becoming a bigger problem by stopping it early, understanding where it comes from, and turning it into resourceful products.

Our focus begins with people living in places affected by flooding and plastic pollution — communities that benefit when surroundings and waterways become cleaner and more resilient.
